BLISS is a Bayesian method for deblending and cataloging light sources in astronomical images. It is developed by the [Probabilistic Machine Learning Research Group](https://github.com/prob-ml) at the University of Michigan. The method is based on a generative model of the astronomical imaging process, and uses a variational Bayesian inference algorithm to estimate the posterior distribution over the number of sources, their properties, and the background noise.
