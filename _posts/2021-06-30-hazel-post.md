[Hazel](https://hazel.org/build/dev/) is a live functional language able to type-check, manipulate and run incomplete programs with "holes". As an undergraduate research member at the [Future of Programming Lab](https://neurocy.notion.site/Future-of-Programming-Lab-241d162461a04064ae1fd9ae32bf4cb1) at the University of Michigan, I worked on Hazel features relating to [operator type prediction](https://github.com/hazelgrove/hazel/pull/459) in arithmetic expressions with holes.
